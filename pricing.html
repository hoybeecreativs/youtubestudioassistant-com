Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

Get a free trial license key, install the extension, then paste the key in the extension popup and click Activate License. You get every feature for 7 days on one device. The trial can be used once per device.

What happens after the trial ends?

The features stop working until you activate a Lifetime License. Your settings stay saved, so you can continue right where you left off.

Do I need my own AI API key?

Not to get started. Pollinations is free and needs no key. For other providers, like Claude, Gemini, OpenAI or Grok, you add your own API key and pay that provider directly for what you use.

How do I receive my license key?

Right after payment, your license key is sent to your email. Paste it into the extension popup and click Activate License.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. A Lifetime License works on up to 2 devices. You can deactivate a device in the extension settings to move your license to another one.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not happy within 30 days of purchase, email support@youtubestudioassistant.com and we'll refund you in full.
